1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to the site owners.

Cookies can be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

We also use similar technologies such as local storage and web beacons for comparable purposes.

6. Google Consent Mode v2

WebZoneBW implements Google Consent Mode v2, which communicates your cookie consent choices to Google before any tracking scripts load.

When you decline analytics or advertising cookies, Consent Mode v2 sends "denied" signals to Google, ensuring that:

  • Google Analytics does not read or write cookies for measurement.
  • Google AdSense does not serve personalized advertisements.
  • Your advertising identifier is not used for profiling.

This means your consent choices are respected at the script level, not just at the UI level.

8. Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete some or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific or all sites
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Accept all cookies
  • Receive a notification when a cookie is set

To manage cookies in your browser, please refer to the help documentation for your specific browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: Disabling cookies in your browser may affect the functionality of WebZoneBW and other websites you visit. Some features may not work correctly if cookies are blocked.
