5. Google AdSense and Advertising

WebZoneBW displays advertisements through Google AdSense.

Google AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to serve advertisements based on a visitor's prior visits to WebZoneBW or other websites on the internet. This practice is known as interest-based advertising.

Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Google may serve personalized ads, which use information about your past browsing behavior to show you more relevant advertisements. Alternatively, Google may serve non-personalized ads, which are not based on your past browsing behavior but may still use general demographic information, your general location, and contextual content of the page being viewed.

Depending on your region, you may be able to manage your ad personalization preferences through your Google Ad Settings .

Third-Party Advertising Partners

Third-party advertising networks and partners serving ads on WebZoneBW may also use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your visits to this website and other websites in order to provide relevant advertisements. These third parties operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

Google's DART Cookie

Google may use the DART cookie to serve advertisements based on your visits to WebZoneBW and other websites on the internet. The DART cookie enables Google to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to WebZoneBW and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of the use of the DART cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page .

Managing and Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

Visitors have several options for managing personalized advertising:

  • Adjust your Google Ad Settings to control ad personalization
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies through the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI)
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings (this may affect website functionality)
  • Install browser extensions that block tracking or advertising cookies
  • Use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent your data from being collected by Google Analytics

Please note that opting out of personalized advertising does not mean you will stop seeing advertisements. It means the advertisements you see may be less relevant to your interests.
