👁️ Safe & Responsible Usage Guide

  • Eye Protection: Take regular breaks during extended lens use. Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
  • Lighting Conditions: Use in well-lit environments to reduce eye strain. Avoid using lenses in complete darkness or extremely bright conditions.
  • Screen Time Management: Limit continuous lens sessions to 30-45 minutes at a time. Your eyes need rest from digital screens.
  • Camera Exposure: Be mindful of camera flash and bright lights. Some effects may increase screen brightness — adjust accordingly.
  • Comfort First: If you experience eye discomfort, headache, or dizziness, stop using lenses immediately and consult an eye care professional.
  • Children & Sensitive Users: Use with caution for children or users with light sensitivity. Parental guidance recommended for younger users.
