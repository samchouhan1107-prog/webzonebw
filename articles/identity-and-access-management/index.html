Identity & Access Management (IAM) Strategy

Identity is the new perimeter in modern IT. As infrastructure moves towards hybrid cloud models, securing *who* has access to *what* becomes paramount.

1. Centralized Identity

At the core of IAM lies the directory service—Active Directory (AD) or Entra ID. Centralizing identity management allows for consistent policy application and easier auditing of user activity.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Implementing RBAC ensures that permissions are assigned based on user roles rather than individual accounts. This simplifies management and significantly reduces the risk of privilege escalation.

3. The Path Forward

For those starting their journey, focus on standardizing your directory structure and mapping user roles effectively. Advanced integration, such as automating account provisioning through APIs connected to AD, is the next logical step in maturing your IAM posture.
